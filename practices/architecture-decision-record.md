# Practice: Architecture Decision Record

* **Identity:** architecture-decision-record
* **Type:** Practice
* **Status:** Draft

---

## Action

Document significant architectural decisions—along with their context, options considered, and trade-offs—in lightweight, version-controlled records kept alongside the codebase.

---

## Foundations & Mapping

* **Realizes Principles:**
  * None established yet.
* **Supported By Mental Models:**
  * `reduce-cognitive-load`: Makes historical design rationale explicit, reducing the amount of context future maintainers must reconstruct.

---

## Boundary

This Practice defines a repeatable approach to documenting architectural decisions as version-controlled artifacts. It does not enforce a specific file format, ADR template, storage tool, or documentation platform.
