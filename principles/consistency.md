# Principle: Consistency

* **Identity:** consistency
* **Type:** Principle
* **Status:** Draft

---

## Claim

Similar things should be done in similar ways, and different things should be done in different ways.

---

## Evidence

* **Source:** `ousterhout-2018`
* **Location:** Chapter 18 ("Consistency"), Page 143
* **Quote Boundary:**
  > "Consistency is a powerful tool for reducing the complexity of a system and making its behavior more predictable. If a system is consistent, it means that similar things are done in similar ways, and different things are done in different ways."

---

## Boundary

This Principle concerns consistency in how similar and different things are treated within a system. It does not require unrelated things to be treated identically or prevent deliberate differences where the underlying problems are different.
